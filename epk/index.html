Bio

LINER NOTES

yuusef is a sydney artist working where shoegaze and alt-rock meet, with a gothic streak running through it: stacked, distorted guitars, heavy low end, wide dynamics and a voice that sits inside the noise rather than on top of it. the aim is the old shoegaze things, spacey vocals, layers on layers, distortion that feels good, with real weight underneath and nothing fighting for space.

the songs are written, mixed and mastered in a room in sydney, often with producers like noise, myss and softscars. since the debut single “when i died in tokyo” in august 2023, yuusef has put out 62 releases, including the albums solitude (2024) and a f t e r l i f e (2026) and the eps seeing through the ceiling and dystopia_ (both 2025).

the catalogue has passed 1.1 million streams with no label behind it. the songs are regulars across solace’s spotify playlists, and yuusef has shared credits with noise, sarm?, lorentø, bre, aprils ghost and moral, among others.
